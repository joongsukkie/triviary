Triviary

Three games. One hive. Every day.

Triviary is a daily trivia game for iPhone. Each day has a theme and three short games:

Stack

Slot each card into the tower in the right order. Three misses and the tower is done.

Over the Edge

Dial in a number as close to the real answer as you dare. Go over and the honey spills.

Hivemind

Type the answer you think most people would give. The more common it is, the more you score.

There is a full year of days, and every past day stays open to play. No account, no ads, no purchases.
