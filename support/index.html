Help with Triviary

How scoring works

Each game is worth up to 100, so a perfect day is 300. In Stack you score for every card placed correctly. In Over the Edge your score is how full the jar gets, and going over the answer scores zero. In Hivemind the most common answer scores 100 and less common answers score less.

My streak did not go up

Streaks count days you finish on the day itself. Past days played from the archive still earn honey and badges, but they do not extend a streak.

My Hivemind answer was not accepted

Each board lists the most common answers. Spelling can be a little off, but an answer that is not on the board scores zero. The boards are our own ranking of what people most often say, not live results from other players.

Can I replay a day?

Yes. Open Past Days, tap a finished day and choose Replay for practice. Practice runs do not change your saved score.

Sound and haptics

Both can be turned off from the person icon on the home screen. Sound follows your phone's silent switch.

Something looks wrong

If you spot a wrong fact or a bug, write to austinshin219@gmail.com with the day number and the question.
