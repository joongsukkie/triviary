Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Triviary does not collect any information about you.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, streak, honey, badges and your sound and haptics settings are saved on your device so the app can show them to you. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them, and so does "Erase all progress" in the app.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics
  • No advertising
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No third-party code that collects data
  • No network requests at all: the questions ship inside the app

Sharing

If you tap Share on a results screen, the app hands a short piece of text to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to you and the app you choose.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
